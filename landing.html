// ARCHITECTURE PILLARS

Designed for High-Traffic Scalability

Every element is optimized for durability, high-efficiency mechanical conversion, and seamless cloud integration.

01 // TRANSDUCTION

Direct Piezoelectric Array

High-sensitivity PZT discs mounted inside hexagonal structural composite plates convert mechanical impact directly into electrical charge with near-zero latency.

  • — High-yield PZT-5H crystal elements
  • — 500,000+ compression test rating
  • — Impact-absorbing rubberized top mat
02 // POWER MANAGEMENT

Intelligent BMS Circuitry

Active TP4056 charge regulation paired with INA219 high-side current and voltage sensing provides over-charge, over-discharge, and thermal protection.

  • — Ultra-low dropout Schottky bridge
  • — Multi-cell Li-ion buffer topology
  • — Regulated 5V USB output rails
03 // IOT & CLOUD

IoT Live Telemetry

Integrated ESP32 Wi-Fi microcontroller continuously streams tile voltages, battery state-of-charge, and footfall events to our Supabase database.

  • — Micro-second hardware interrupt triggers
  • — Deep sleep power management
  • — Real-time User & Admin dashboards
// ENERGY LIFECYCLE

From Footstep to Usable Power

Trace the millisecond conversion pathway that brings kinetic energy into battery storage.

01

Mechanical Deflection

A pedestrian step imparts dynamic mechanical pressure on the hexagonal matrix.

02

High-Frequency Spike

PZT transducers generate an instantaneous AC voltage spike of up to 20V.

03

Rectification & BMS

Low-loss diodes rectify AC to DC, buffering current into regulated Li-ion storage.

04

IoT Dashboard Sync

ESP32 logs the telemetry payload directly to our cloud database.
