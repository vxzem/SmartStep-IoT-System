🎯 General Objective

To design, fabricate, and empirically validate an IoT-integrated piezoelectric footstep energy harvesting floor system capable of transforming pedestrian kinetic motion into regulated micro-power to address localized electrical deficits in off-grid Philippine communities.

🔍 Specific Research Objectives

  • 1 Modular PZT Transducer Matrix: Model compression stress-strain distribution across hexagonal tile casings to maximize voltage yield per step.
  • 2 Low-Loss BMS Regulation: Engineer rectification and battery management circuits capable of capturing rapid microsecond voltage spikes safely.
  • 3 Edge Cloud Telemetry: Stream continuous footfall count, state-of-charge, and node health data to Supabase for automated maintenance.

⚠️ Scope & Engineering Limitations

The SmartStep prototype is engineered specifically for localized low-wattage emergency infrastructure — including path illumination, auxiliary USB mobile communication charging, and IoT remote environmental sensors. It is not intended for inductive high-current industrial appliances (such as compressors or high-draw refrigeration). Data logging requires local Wi-Fi or cellular gateway handshake for persistent cloud telemetry sync.
