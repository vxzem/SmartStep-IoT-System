// CAPSTONE INITIATIVE // TEAM 404

Bridging the Gap in Renewable Energy

Developing decentralized, self-powering piezoelectric floor tiles to alleviate power deficits in off-grid facilities and smart urban transit corridors.

24.45%
Energy Loss in Traditional Micro-Grids

Centralized diesel generators in island communities suffer from high fuel logistics overhead, voltage drop, and carbon emissions while continuous foot traffic kinetic energy remains entirely uncaptured.

The Off-Grid Challenge

Traditional energy generation systems struggle with geographical isolation. In island settings such as Gilutongan Island, residents experience intermittent electrical access, limiting essential educational equipment and community lighting.

SmartStep was created by Team 404 to convert everyday mechanical locomotion into a sustainable, localized micropower source — decentralizing generation to where people actually walk.

Target Field Pilot: Gilutongan Island Community Center

Designed for public footways, school hallways, and multi-purpose civic plazas.

The SmartStep Innovation

By leveraging the direct piezoelectric effect of lead zirconate titanate (PZT) ceramic transducers, each footstep generates an electric polarization that is captured, rectified, and stored in regulated buffer cells.

🌱

Zero-Carbon Kinetic Harvesting

Produces usable electrical power entirely from passive human movement without reliance on weather or fossil fuels.

🌐

// ROADMAP & LIFECYCLE

Capstone Development Journey

From theoretical piezoelectric formulations to working IoT prototypes.

PHASE 01

Transducer Feasibility

Bench-testing PZT ceramic discs under varying weight loads (40kg–100kg) to map voltage generation curves.

PHASE 02

Rectification & BMS

Designed ultra-low-loss full-wave Schottky bridge circuits and integrated TP4056 lithium battery buffers.

PHASE 03

IoT & Cloud Dashboards

Programmed ESP8266 microcontroller firmware to stream telemetry via Supabase Edge and Realtime websockets.

PHASE 04

Island Pilot Testing

Validating real-world tile wear, foot traffic count accuracy, and localized LED illumination reliability.
