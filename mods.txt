Administrator

1.1 Access Control

    Authentication: Secure Admin Login and Session Management (Log-out).

1.2 Real-Time Node Monitoring

    Sensor-Level SoC: Granular view of energy generated by individual piezoelectric sensors.

    Storage Diagnostics: Live monitoring of Battery Voltage and overall State of Charge (SoC).

    Efficiency Tracking: Real-time ratio of energy harvested versus parasitic power loss.

    Footfall Metrics: Live "Today’s Steps" counter and pedestrian frequency tracking.

    I/O Status: Monitoring the 5V charging port activity (Detects if a device is charging).

    Hardware Integrity: Real-time status alerts for the mat’s physical and electrical condition.

1.3 Advanced Analytics Hub

    Forecasting Engine: Historical data views with time-series forecasting (Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Annual).

    Peak Analysis: Identification of highest charging rates and busiest pedestrian hours.

    Energy Audit: Comparative reports of total energy generated vs. total energy consumed by mobile devices.

1.4 Maintenance & Hardware Configuration

    Remote Firmware Commands: Over-the-air (OTA) system restarts and sensor recalibration.

    Battery Management: Advanced diagnostics and health indexing.

    Threshold Tuning: Configuration of safety limits (Low-Voltage Cut-off, High-Voltage Alerts).

    Direct Support: A dedicated communication box to send maintenance or repair alerts to specific users.

1.5 System Settings

    Global Preferences: Backup management and system-wide synchronization.

    Hardware Provisioning: Interface to register and pair new User-Mat combinations.

General User Dashboard


2.1 User Access

    Authentication: Standard User Login/Registration and Log-out.

2.2 Interactive Personal Dashboard

    Energy Flow Visualization: Real-time graphic showing current battery level and power availability.

    The "Green" Footprint: Personal "Today’s Steps" counter to visualize individual contribution.

    Device Status: Instant feedback on charging status when a phone is connected.

2.3 Community Analytics

    Sustainability Scoreboard: Community-wide view of total energy harvested (Weekly/Monthly rankings).

    Smart Charging Guide: Information on peak usage hours to help users find the best time to charge.

2.4 Personalization & Preferences

    Account Management: Profile updates and security settings.

    Notification Controls: Toggle preferences for Push or Email alerts.

    Node Self-Service: Ability for users to trigger a restart or recalibration for their specific mat if permitted.

2.5 Notification Center

    Alert Feed: Centralized hub for system updates, maintenance news, or performance milestones.

    Inbox Tools: Filters (All/Unread) and bulk actions (Mark as Read/Clear All).
